Scope and limitationsThis tool reproduces and extends the DFO End-of-Pipe Screen Size Tool's sizing logic. It is an interim standard applied under a regulatory instrument (e.g. Fisheries Act Authorization) with its own conditions. It does not address risks from physical pipe installation or withdrawal volume, may not apply to large intakes/high withdrawal rates, and does not remove obligations under the Fisheries Act, Species at Risk Act, or other law. The design approach velocity must be derived from species swim performance (SPOT); this tool does not model swim performance. A Qualified Environmental Professional (QEP) determines site-specific methods. Site-specific factors may impose additional requirements.
